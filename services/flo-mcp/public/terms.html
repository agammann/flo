Flo is an open-source development prototype operated by Alexander Ammann. This customer preview is for invited testing with fictional repair records, not a live repair-shop service. Do not enter real customer records, financial details or safety-critical information.

Signing in with Amazon identifies your account. It does not establish ownership of repairs. Access is limited to records separately associated with you by the operator. Do not attempt to access another person's information.

Repair and estimate review does not approve work, create a booking, order parts or charge money. Nothing in this preview is a repair diagnosis or a commitment by a real shop. The preview may be changed, interrupted or retired. It is not certified by Alexa+.
